Distance vs pumps

Keyboard: Space pumps, Enter releases, R starts over. The physics is a simple model built from published measurements (see Sources); distances are estimates, not measurements of your string.

Where the numbers come from

The sim uses standard equations for balloon pressure, jet thrust, air resistance and friction. Every equation and constant comes from these sources, except three: the balloon size and its dry mass are estimates for a 12 inch latex balloon, and the string friction is tuned so distances match classroom strings.
